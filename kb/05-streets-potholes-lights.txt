Streets, potholes, and streetlights
Public-works reporting for the fictional City of Northbridge.

Q: How do I report a pothole?
A: Tell 311 the nearest address or cross streets, the lane (travel, bike, or parking), and the size if you can. Photos help but are optional. You will get an NB-###### request number. Arterial streets (Harbor Boulevard, College Avenue, Civic Way, Airport Road) have a 3-business-day patch target. Neighborhood streets have a 10-business-day target.

Q: How do I report a streetlight that is out?
A: Give the pole number if it is visible on the metal tag, plus the nearest address. If several lights on a block are dark, say so — that can be a circuit issue. Target response is 5 business days after the pole is identified.

Q: What about traffic signals and stop signs?
A: A dark or stuck traffic signal is treated as urgent. If the intersection is unsafe, call 911. For a bent or missing stop sign, call 311 and describe the intersection. Temporary signs are often placed the same business day.

Q: Can 311 help with sidewalk trip hazards?
A: Yes. Report the address and whether the panel is lifted, cracked, or blocked. Sidewalks next to city property are a city repair. Sidewalks next to private property may be the adjacent owner’s responsibility; 311 will explain after a field check.

Q: Who owns the street in front of my house?
A: Most neighborhood streets are city-maintained. Harbor Boulevard north of the river and the airport connector are county routes. 311 will not open an NB- work order for a county route; staff will give you the county roads number instead.
