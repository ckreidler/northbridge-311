Parks and recreation
Park hours and reservations for the fictional City of Northbridge.

Q: What are park hours?
A: Neighborhood parks are open dawn to dusk unless a posted event says otherwise. Lighted athletic fields may stay open until 10:00 p.m. when a permitted game is scheduled. Riverfront Walk is open 5:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m.

Q: Which parks have picnic shelters?
A: Maplewood Park has two shelters, a playground, and restrooms. Lakeview Park has one lakeside shelter and a fishing pier. Old Mill Green has one shelter and no grills. Reserve a shelter at least 7 days ahead through 311 or the parks desk. Resident fee (demo): $35 half day, $60 full day. You will receive an NB-###### reservation number.

Q: Are dogs allowed?
A: Dogs are welcome on leash in most parks. They are not allowed on athletic turf or inside playground fences. Lakeview Off-Leash Meadow is the only fenced dog area. Owners must pick up waste.

Q: How do I report a park issue?
A: Call 311 for broken playground equipment, overflowing trash, graffiti, or a locked restroom during posted hours. Give the park name and the nearest landmark. After-hours safety threats should go to 911.

Q: Does the city have a community pool?
A: Southridge Pool is open mid-June through mid-August, 12:00 p.m. to 6:00 p.m. daily, weather permitting. Lap swim is 7:00 to 8:30 a.m. on weekdays. Season passes are sold at Civic Plaza recreation.

Q: Can I fly a drone in a city park?
A: Recreational drones are not allowed over playgrounds, pools, or permitted events. Launching from Riverfront Walk requires a parks special-use permit.
