Service request basics

How Northbridge 311 opens, numbers, and tracks service requests.

Q: What does a Northbridge request number look like?

A: Every accepted request gets a number in the form NB-###### — the letters NB, a hyphen, and six digits. Example: NB-104822. Always keep this number. You need it to check status or add photos later.

Q: How do I open a service request?

A: Provide:

  1. The type of issue (for example, missed trash or a pothole)
  2. A street address or nearest intersection in Northbridge
  3. What you observed and when
  4. A callback number or email if you want updates

You can open a request by phone, website form, or chat. You do not need a city account for most request types.

Q: How do I check status?

A: Give 311 the request number (NB-######). Typical statuses are Received, Assigned, In progress, On hold (need more information), Scheduled, and Closed.

Q: How long do requests take?

  • Missed trash or recycling: next scheduled collection day after the report is confirmed
  • Potholes on arterial streets: target 3 business days
  • Residential potholes: target 10 business days
  • Streetlight outages: target 5 business days after the pole number is confirmed
  • Permit questions: same business day during lobby hours if the queue allows

A: These are service targets, not guarantees. Weather, parts, and safety issues can extend timelines.

Q: Can I update or cancel a request?

A: Yes. Call or chat with the NB-###### number. You can add details, a better location, or a photo description. Cancel only if the issue is already resolved or was opened by mistake.

Q: What if I do not live in Northbridge?

A: 311 can only create work orders for locations inside city limits. For county roads or a neighboring town, 311 will tell you which agency to contact instead of opening an NB- request.
