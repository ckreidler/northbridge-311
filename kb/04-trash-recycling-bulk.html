Trash, recycling, and bulk pickup

Solid waste rules for the fictional City of Northbridge.

Q: What is my collection day?

A: Collection day is based on city ward:

  • Ward 1 (Riverfront, Old Mill): Monday
  • Ward 2 (College Hill, Station District): Tuesday
  • Ward 3 (Maplewood, Civic Plaza): Wednesday
  • Ward 4 (Westbrook, Airport Road): Thursday
  • Ward 5 (Southridge, Lakeview): Friday

If you do not know your ward, give 311 your street address.

Q: What time should carts be at the curb?

A: Place carts at the curb by 6:00 a.m. on collection day, wheels toward the house, lid closed. Bring carts back off the street by 8:00 p.m. the same day.

Q: What goes in recycling?

A: Northbridge accepts clean paper, flattened cardboard, metal cans, glass bottles, and plastic bottles and jugs labeled #1 or #2. Do not bag recyclables. Rinse food containers.

Do not recycle plastic bags, foam, tangled cords, or food-soaked paper. Plastic bags can be returned to many grocery stores.

Q: How do I report a missed pickup?

A: Wait until 5:00 p.m. on your collection day, then contact 311 with your address and whether trash, recycling, or yard waste was missed. 311 opens an NB-###### request. Confirmed misses are usually collected the next business day.

Q: How does bulk-item pickup work?

A: Each household gets four scheduled bulk pickups per calendar year. Call 311 at least five business days ahead. Set out up to five items: furniture, mattresses (bagged), or appliances with refrigerant removed. No construction debris, tires, or household hazardous waste.

Q: What about yard waste and extra bags?

A: Yard-waste carts are collected April through November on the same day as trash. Extra trash bags need a Northbridge pay-as-you-throw sticker from the Civic Plaza shop or a participating hardware store.
