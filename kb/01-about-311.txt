About Northbridge 311
City of Northbridge (fictional portfolio city). 311 is the non-emergency front door for city services.

Q: What is Northbridge 311?
A: Northbridge 311 is the city’s single place to ask questions and open service requests for non-emergency issues. Residents can call 311, use the 311 website, or chat with the city services assistant.

Q: When should I call 911 instead of 311?
A: Call 911 for crime in progress, fire, medical emergencies, gas leaks you can smell inside a building, or any situation that is life-threatening. 311 cannot dispatch police, fire, or ambulance.

Q: What can 311 help with?
A: Common topics include trash, recycling, yard waste, and bulk-item pickup; potholes, streetlights, signs, and sidewalk hazards; water billing questions and non-emergency leaks; building, right-of-way, and event permits; park hours, picnic shelters, and recreation programs; and status checks on an existing request number (NB-######).

Q: How do I reach 311?
A: Inside city limits, dial 311. From outside Northbridge, dial 555-0100 (demo number). Online chat is available on the 311 website during published hours.

Q: Where is the 311 service center?
A: City of Northbridge, 100 Civic Plaza, Northbridge. Walk-in service is at the Civic Plaza first-floor service counter during weekday lobby hours.
