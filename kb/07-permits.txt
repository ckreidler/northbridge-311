Permits
Common permit types for the fictional City of Northbridge.

Q: What permits does 311 handle questions about?
A: 311 can explain how to apply and where to drop materials for building, electrical, plumbing, and mechanical permits; right-of-way / dumpster / driveway-apron permits; special event and block-party permits; and park picnic-shelter reservations. 311 does not issue state licenses (driver, business tax ID, or professional licenses).

Q: Do I need a building permit for a backyard shed?
A: In Northbridge, a shed of 120 square feet or less that is not on a permanent foundation and is set back at least 5 feet from property lines usually does not need a building permit. Larger sheds, any electrical service, or a shed used as living space needs a permit. When in doubt, ask Building Review before you buy materials.

Q: How long does a simple building permit take?
A: Over-the-counter reviews for water-heater swaps and like-for-like window replacements are often same day if you arrive before 3:00 p.m. on a weekday. Small residential additions target 10 business days. Commercial projects vary.

Q: How do I get a dumpster on the street?
A: Apply for a right-of-way permit at least 3 business days before delivery. You need the address, dates, and dumpster size. The permit is not a parking reservation on posted tow-away routes.

Q: How do I apply for a block party?
A: Submit the special-event form at least 15 days ahead, with a petition from most addresses on the closed block. 311 can open an NB-###### tracking number for the application. Police and Fire review street closures.

Q: Where do I submit plans?
A: Digital submittals go through the Northbridge Permit Portal (demo). Paper sets can be dropped at the Civic Plaza second-floor permit counter, Monday through Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
