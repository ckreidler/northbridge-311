FAQ quick answers
Short answers the Northbridge 311 assistant can use first.

Q: What is the 311 phone number?
A: Dial 311 inside the city. From outside Northbridge, dial 555-0100 (demo).

Q: What is a request number?
A: Northbridge request numbers look like NB-###### (example: NB-104822).

Q: Is this a real city?
A: No. City of Northbridge is a fictional portfolio demo for Amazon Connect / ACXD.

Q: When is 311 open?
A: Monday–Friday 7:00 a.m.–7:00 p.m., Saturday 8:00 a.m.–4:00 p.m., Sunday closed. Holidays follow the city calendar.

Q: What is trash day in Ward 3?
A: Wednesday. Carts out by 6:00 a.m.

Q: How do I report a pothole?
A: Give 311 the nearest address or intersection. You will get an NB-###### number.

Q: Streetlight is out. What do I need?
A: Pole number (metal tag) and nearest address. Target 5 business days after the pole is identified.

Q: Water in the street. Who do I call?
A: Call 311. After hours it routes to on-call utilities. Smell of gas = 911.

Q: Do I need a permit for a small shed?
A: Often no if it is 120 square feet or less, not on a permanent foundation, and at least 5 feet from property lines. Ask Building Review if you are unsure.

Q: Can I reserve a picnic shelter?
A: Yes, at least 7 days ahead. Maplewood, Lakeview, and Old Mill Green have shelters. Resident demo fees: $35 half day, $60 full day.

Q: What languages are available?
A: English and Spanish directly. Portuguese and Mandarin by interpreter.

Q: I want a person.
A: Ask for a specialist or supervisor. 311 will escalate and can take a callback number.
