Hours, holidays, and languages

Published hours for the fictional City of Northbridge 311 service center.

Q: What are 311 phone and chat hours?

  • Monday–Friday: 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. local time
  • Saturday: 8:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.
  • Sunday: closed for non-emergency intake

A: After hours, residents can still leave a voicemail or submit a web request. Messages are reviewed the next business morning.

Q: What are Civic Plaza lobby hours?

A: Walk-in counter hours are Monday–Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. The lobby is closed on weekends and city holidays.

Q: Which holidays is 311 closed?

A: Northbridge 311 follows the city holiday calendar:

  • New Year’s Day
  • Martin Luther King Jr. Day
  • Presidents’ Day
  • Memorial Day
  • Juneteenth
  • Independence Day
  • Labor Day
  • Veterans Day
  • Thanksgiving Day
  • Day after Thanksgiving
  • Christmas Day

When a holiday falls on a Saturday, the city observes the Friday before. When it falls on a Sunday, the city observes the Monday after.

Q: What languages does 311 support?

A: Specialists and the digital assistant can help in English and Spanish. Portuguese and Mandarin are available through a phone interpreter. Ask for an interpreter at the start of the call or chat.

Q: Does trash collection run on holidays?

A: If your collection day falls on a city holiday, pickup usually moves to the next business day. Recycling and yard-waste follow the same holiday shift. Check the trash topic for Ward-by-Ward days.
